# Visible celestial paths across backgrounds

The project-authored SVG mosque-sky-mask.svg traces a conservative union of the existing day/night mountain, dome and minaret silhouettes in the original 1536×1024 source frame. It imports no external geometry or raster data. Both existing photos remain byte-identical. By explicit user preference, the decorative crescent in night-mosque.webp stays; the dynamic Moon is suppressed only on this wallpaper.

Sun/Moon astronomy and time-based visibility are unchanged. A pure display projection maps the day arc across the visible portion of each photo and fits elevation above its own skyline. A per-artwork horizon accounts for mountain and city scenes and paired tablet artwork. One source rectangle keeps masks, sky and photo aligned, while projected centres stay within phone/tablet crops. Object sizes are bounded. No new network service, data collection, location change or private access.

Validation includes continuous projection and bounds for all23cataloguecities plus mosque/NewYork in portrait/landscape phones and tablets, with explicit no-duplicate-moon checks. Original mosque imagery is preserved; an unused experimental removal output is outside the app and is not shipped.
