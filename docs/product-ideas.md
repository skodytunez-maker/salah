## Player sleep timer and optional nature sounds — 13 November 2026

Proposed by the owner: a timer and optional rain, wind, campfire, forest and ocean sounds alongside Quran recitation. Suggested controls live inside the expanded player, with separate background volume, disabled by default. Timer presets: 15, 30, 45, 60 minutes and end of the current surah. Nature sound alone must never add Quran listening time. Prepared for 485: stop the background with Quran pause/timer, keep it off by default and save only its separate volume. Verify locked-screen playback, real stop timing and interruptions on iPhone and Android before promising background reliability. This idea is recorded for a separate player change; SALAH 484 does not implement or announce it.
