# SALAH 482 — Home controls

Home owner authorization was coupled to an unread release card: dismissing news stopped renewal, and a stored receipt skipped verification after a fresh launch. After the 60-second grant expired the activity row became noninteractive and the radio beacon disappeared.

The Home controller now checks a signed-in account once, keeps one 45-second renewal only for a verified owner, and stops on leaving Home, hiding the document or signing out. Acknowledging news hides the notice without cancelling owner controls. Expired authority is never extended by a token refresh alone; a successful server recheck publishes the restored authority immediately. Protected participant lists and the radio route continue to verify current owner authority and MFA.

Activity icons receive a larger invisible touch target without moving the prayer group. The closed radio beacon stays at the top left, with the iPhone safe inset included; ordinary accounts have no radio entry. The approved 20px prayer-group shift, right-hand activity alignment, player and navigation positions remain unchanged. No new public news; announcement479, acknowledgement and expiry are retained. No server changes.
