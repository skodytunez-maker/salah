# SALAH product requirements

- Preserve confirmed fixes with regressions for the reported user behavior: single Adhkar label/count badges across all interface languages, authoritative player pause despite delayed callbacks, player dismissal returning to its originating route, and fresh installed launches opening Home. Keep before/after evidence, verify the exact candidate source and rerun affected checks before every release. A fixture pass does not replace the owner's physical-phone approval.

- The owner explicitly authorized public513 to combine the basic day/night photo and language-facing corrections with the player pause, player dismissal/navigation, Adhkar label/count, and fresh-launch Home fixes for all users. Do not release512 separately. This exception does not authorize live sky: keep ADVANCED_SKY_AVAILABLE false, hide its controls/demos and fail closed on saved preferences or direct preview calls. Only the new living sky is owner-private until explicit post-phone-test approval.

- Every future release candidate must be tested on the owner's phone first. Publish or roll out that candidate to other users only after the owner explicitly approves it following the phone test. Preparing code, isolated preview builds and checks does not authorize a global release, changing the public latest manifest or merging a branch that automatically publishes. Preserve the currently approved public version while preparing a pilot.
- Home's default is the original sharp day/night photographs with a simple gradual light transition. The new advanced living sky must require an explicit opt-in in Atmosphere settings, defaulting off for new, existing and invalid stored preferences. Keep its expensive preparation, celestial assets, canvas work and motion off in basic mode; a temporary explicit demonstration must not silently save opt-in.

- Batch minor fixes into fewer releases. The owner wants fewer external update pushes in the iPhone notification center; do not send a separate update push for every small fix. Preserve the existing in-app update notices. This preference does not authorize a new external message or change religious reminders.
- Keep each requested fix within its named section and review its exact file scope before release. Preserve the approved appearance and behavior of other sections. If a necessary dependency requires changing another section, tell the owner what and why before making that change; this is a notification requirement, not an invented approval flow. Do not add unsolicited labels, sources, controls or information to unrelated screens. In particular, keep source/license text out of the About card under the Instagram links; preserve source files and existing content attributions where they belong.
- Every release must pass the live-listening regression for the shared total and own portrait, including delayed server acknowledgements, pauses, remounts, multiple reciters, guests and account transfer. Keep the reviewed file-scope manifest current and verify unrelated files against its baseline. A passing unit fixture does not establish which version is running on a phone; check release/version evidence before treating an old-client symptom as a new regression.
- Home live weather keeps natural celestial visibility: dense clouds and fog hide the Sun and Moon; do not force a decorative Sun through overcast weather. Draw visible cloud structure in the sky while preserving sharp photographs, architecture and courtyard detail. Avoid a uniform grey veil over the whole picture and preserve the fix for rectangular skyline halos.
- Home dawn, daylight, sunset and night share one coherent atmosphere on every available wallpaper, including artwork with little sky. Preserve photographic foreground detail and each day/night source's own framing. A source without a verified matte keeps its complete decoded photograph; never reveal coarse rectangular cutouts as an opaque sky.
- Sky variation follows the local date, location and weather deterministically, stays continuous across midnight, and does not change randomly on reopening. Respect hidden/reduced-motion/offline lifecycles and keep all motion work bounded. A photographic atmosphere is an illustration of local conditions, not an exact view of the current clouds or a calibrated camera bearing.

- Public release notes must show feature-specific pictures with short explanations in the existing gallery. This is a standing user preference. Do not substitute a generic book, star, or play icon for the feature illustration.
- A new announcement must include only newly introduced changes and feature-specific pictures for that edition. Do not append older reciter, Qibla or other previously announced cards. Preserve cards only within the same current edition. Keep acknowledgement and expiry unchanged for minor fixes. Update docs/release-announcements.md and use release-artwork validation.
- Minor fixes keep the current announcement identifier and expiry. After «Понятно», Home news stays dismissed for that announcement; Menu may reopen it. Do not reset acknowledgement for cosmetic changes.
- Animated release cards use the shared `release-motion-preview` loop with `data-release-motion-part` for each moving element. Alternate directions while the active card is open, except Sun/Moon sky previews: these follow one rise-to-set arc and restart only while hidden below the horizon. Pause hidden cards and respect reduced motion. In the installed news dialog, record acknowledgement only after «Понятно».
- Before publishing, run scripts/check-release-illustrations.mjs. Every current public change must be assigned to appropriate feature artwork; add artwork for an unsupported feature.

- Do not announce subscriptions, monthly financial support, billing or the YooKassa review/cancellation flow in public news until the user explicitly asks to announce them. This includes current, waiting-update and legacy release cards. Preserve the private review link and payment UI.

- A user's prayer city, country, and geography derived from user locations are private. Other users’ location and geography reports are visible only to the verified owner through owner-only endpoints with active session and MFA. A user may view or change their own prayer city. Public rankings, public profiles, staff and support views must not expose another user's location.

- Tafsir is temporarily removed from public use at the user's request. Do not restore public Tafsir entry points or promote its availability without an explicit new request.
- Deferred product ideas are recorded in docs/product-ideas.md; recording an idea does not authorize implementation.

- Home activity is anonymous for ordinary users: only aggregate listening, Quran reading and dhikr counts are public. Only the verified owner, through an endpoint requiring an active session and MFA, may open participant lists. Unregistered guests have no known name and must stay labelled as guests.
